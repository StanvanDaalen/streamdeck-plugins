While playing

Style

When the app plays something, the dial shows the title, picture, play/pause and a timeline. Turning briefly shows the volume. Off keeps the normal display.

App indicator

So you can tell this dial apart from Spotify: Spotify shows green, a browser the colour of what it plays (YouTube red, Netflix red, Disney+ blue, …). Logo adds a small black-and-white logo (Player and Picture). Other apps stay white.

Chrome extension

Chrome only reports the service's name: load the extension below.

Netflix and some other services only tell Windows their name. The Stream Deck Now Playing extension passes on the episode, series and picture. Load it once in Chrome: open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose this folder (or paste the copied path). Then refresh the streaming tab. After a plugin update, click Reload on the extension.

Streaming

Series whose service shows no picture (Paramount+, …) get the episode's picture from TVmaze; F1 TV, MotoGP and WEC get the flag of the race weekend from flagcdn.com. Only the series name or country code is sent.

Controls

Hold and turn: rewind or fast-forward this much per step while the app plays something.
