Display

Style

Player looks like the official Spotify dial. Picture shows the cover across the whole screen. Compact shows the Spotify logo with the title.

App indicator

So you can tell this dial apart from your other media dials: the play button or the timeline in Spotify green, or a small black-and-white logo (Player and Picture).

Turning

Controls

Set double- or triple-click to Nothing and the dial reacts faster. Long-press the screen: left = previous, right = next. If Spotify is closed, any action starts it.
